Withdrawal and refunds

PageBite · Last updated: 15 November 2026

The German version of this page is the legally binding one. This English version is provided for your convenience.

This page explains how to get your money back for PageBite Pro. It depends on where you bought it: on the web at start.pagebite.eu, or in the app through the App Store or Google Play.

1. Buying on the web (Paddle)

On the web, our online reseller Paddle.com sells PageBite Pro. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our web orders and pays out refunds.

14 days money back after the first payment

  • If you ask for your money back within 14 days of the first payment of a subscription, you get the full amount of that payment back. You don't need to give a reason.
  • For the yearly plan with a free trial, the first payment is the one at the end of the trial. So the 14 days only start when you pay for the first time.
  • The refund ends the subscription at once. PageBite Pro is then no longer unlocked. Your cards are kept.
  • We don't refund later renewals unless the law requires it. If you cancel before a renewal, nothing more is charged.

How to get your money back

  • Open Paddle's confirmation email and tap the link to manage your subscription. There you can request a refund or your withdrawal. You can also do this on paddle.net.
  • Or write to us at info@pagebite.eu. Tell us the email address you bought with. We pass your request on to Paddle.
  • Paddle pays the amount back to the payment method you used.

Your statutory right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU, you also have a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days. Your contract for the purchase is with Paddle. Paddle tells you about this right during checkout and in its Buyer Terms. Our rule above does not limit this right.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current period. How to do it is in the terms of use, section 9.2.

2. Buying in the app (App Store, Google Play)

In the app you buy from Apple or Google. Their terms therefore apply to your right of withdrawal and to refunds. You request a refund there. If you have questions, info@pagebite.eu can help.

3. Your other rights

Your statutory rights, for example if PageBite does not work as described, remain unaffected.
