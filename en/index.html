How it works

Three steps from the page into your head.

You read like always. PageBite turns the page into something you can learn with.

  1. Scan a page

    Take photos of one to five pages from your book. The app reads the text on your phone.

  2. Key ideaAI-generated

    You don’t need a faraway place to find calm. You can retreat into yourself at any time.

    Where does Marcus say you can best retreat?

    1. To the country
    2. Into your own soul
    3. To the sea-shore
    Correct!

    Key idea and quiz

    An AI sums up the key idea and asks you three quiz questions about it. Everything is marked as "AI-generated".

  3. QuestionAI-generated

    When can you retire into yourself, according to Marcus?

    Answer

    Whenever you choose.

Learn before you scroll.

Put a lock on distracting apps. Before they open, you answer a few key questions from your own pages.

  1. You open a distracting app.
  2. First you see the PageBite lock with a few key questions.
  3. The app is open for a while, then it locks again.

iPhone only, using your iPhone’s Screen Time. PageBite never learns which apps you pick. You can turn the lock off anytime.

Daily reminder

Once a day, at the time you pick. The reminder comes straight from your phone.

Your books

Every page goes to its book. A photo of the cover stays on your phone as a small picture.

Works offline

Reviews work without internet. The app only needs a connection to make new cards.

In your language

The app is available in English and German. Key ideas, quizzes and flashcards stay in the language of the scanned page.

Your data

Your pages stay yours.

Photos stay on your phone

Page photos are deleted as soon as the text is read. A book cover is kept as a small picture.

Processed in the EU

Only the recognized text is processed in the EU to create your cards.

No account needed

You don’t need an account with an email address. Backup with an email code is optional.

Statistics only with a yes

Usage statistics only if you agree.

All details are in the privacy policy.

Subscription

Your first page is free.

PageBite comes as a yearly plan, which can include a free trial, or as a monthly plan. You see the prices and the trial length in the app before you buy. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play settings.
