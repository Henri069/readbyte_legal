Privacy policy

PageBite · Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

  • Photos of your book pages stay on your phone. They are deleted as soon as the text is read, or, if you mark passages on the photo, as soon as you are done.
  • Only the recognized text is sent briefly to Google Gemini (Google Cloud, processed in the EU), together with the characters, terms and flashcard questions PageBite already has from the book, to create the key idea, quiz and flashcards. The original text stays on your phone.
  • You don't have to register. The app creates an anonymous account. Signing in with an email code is optional.
  • In the app we only collect usage statistics if you agree. On start.pagebite.eu we count which steps visitors reach and measure with Meta and TikTok what our ads and videos bring, all only if you agree. Never page texts, card texts or book titles.
  • Your cards are stored on servers in the EU (Frankfurt am Main, Germany).

The German version of this policy is the legally binding one. This English version is provided for your convenience.

When you scan your free page in onboarding, or at the latest right before you see the subscription options, the app creates an anonymous account with our service provider Supabase. We automatically delete anonymous accounts without a subscription after 30 days. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

What your reading list can reveal

The books you read can reveal something about your health, your beliefs or your political views. That is why we protect this data especially well: it never goes into usage statistics, the original text stays on your phone, the servers are in the EU, access is strictly limited to your account, and you can delete everything at any time.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app plans the notifications on your phone only, using the notification feature of iOS or Android. They say how many cards are due and name the books they come from, never what is on your cards. Because notifications can show on the lock screen, you can hide the book titles in Settings. We do not use push notifications, and none of this is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in Settings at any time.

Learning streak

The app counts the days on which you learn and shows your learning streak. For each learning day it only stores the date and the length of your streak. Like your cards, this is backed up with your account, so your streak continues on a new phone. Whether you had a day without a subscription is only kept on your phone. None of this goes into usage statistics.

4. Scanning: photos stay on your phone

  • For scanning, PageBite uses your phone's document scanner: Apple VisionKit on iPhone, Google's ML Kit Document Scanner on Android. Text recognition also runs on the device (Apple Vision or Google ML Kit).
  • We never upload the photos. The app deletes every page photo as soon as the text is read, even if text recognition fails.
  • Marking passages (on iPhones with iOS 17 or later): so you can mark passages right on the photo, the photos of a scan stay on your phone until you save the page, drop the scan or close the screen. Then the app deletes them. Marking uses Apple's text recognition on the device (Live Text). The marked passages are text of the page: they stay on your phone and only go to Google Gemini with the page text (see section 5).
  • If you scan a book cover, the app keeps only a small picture of it on your phone and deletes the photo right away. The cover is not uploaded and not synced to other devices. The app sends the recognized text of the cover to Google Gemini, so you don't have to type title and author and the app can suggest whether it is non-fiction or fiction (see section 5). Our server does not store this text.
  • On Android, Google ML Kit sends technical data to Google, for example the device model, app version, performance values and error codes. ML Kit does not send images or texts.
  • The app only uses the camera when you start a scan.

5. AI processing with Google Gemini

After your first photo, the app asks for your permission before it sends the recognized text. Only then is the recognized text of a page sent to our server and from there to Google Gemini. If you choose "Not now", nothing is sent, and the text stays on your phone only until you leave the screen. Google Gemini creates the key idea, quiz and flashcards from it. Our server does not store the page text and does not write it into any logs. The same applies to imported Kindle highlights (see section 6).

Together with the page text go the kind of book (non-fiction or fiction) and the characters or terms PageBite already knows from this book: for each one its name and a short description, at most 50. They were themselves made by the AI, from pages you scanned earlier. This lets Gemini add to them instead of creating them twice. Earlier page texts and the book title are not sent. For the free page in onboarding there is no book yet, so Gemini decides from the page whether it comes from a novel.

Since 23 October 2026 the questions of the flashcards the AI already made for this book go along as well: at most 50, the newest first, without the answers. This way Gemini writes no flashcard about a point an earlier card already asks about. Flashcards you wrote yourself are not sent.

In onboarding you can scan one page for free before you subscribe. Until you buy, the result stays on your phone only. So that each account gets only one free page, our server stores your account identifier and the time. We delete this record with your account. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in limiting abuse and costs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

  • Provider: Google Cloud (Vertex AI), Gemini model. Processing in the EU (location eu).
  • No training: Google does not use your texts to train AI models.
  • Storage at Google: Request and response logging is off, and caching of requests is switched off for our Google project, so Google does not keep your text after the answer. Google may store a request for up to 90 days in the EU if automated safety filters flag it as possible abuse.

6. Importing highlights from the Kindle

  • When you start the import, the app opens Amazon's own notebook page (read.amazon.com or lesen.amazon.de) in a browser window inside the app. You log in to Amazon there yourself. You type your password only into Amazon's page. PageBite does not read it and does not store it.
  • The window is private: your Amazon login is not kept in the app and ends when you close the window.
  • On the notebook page, the app reads the list of your Kindle books (title, author, Amazon's id of the book, date of the last highlight). For the books you pick, it reads the highlights (text, page or location, colour, your note, Amazon's id of the highlight). The app does not store your Amazon customer number.
  • Highlights and notes stay only on your phone. We do not store them on our server and do not sync them to other devices. With each card we store only Amazon's id of its highlight, a string of letters and digits without any text. When you import again on a new phone, the app uses it to put each highlight back on its card, without the AI and without duplicate cards.
  • The app sends the text of the highlights in batches to Google Gemini so the AI writes one flashcard per highlight (see section 5). Your notes are not sent. Afterwards our server holds only the cards, in the AI's own words, as for scanned pages, and the title and author of the book, as for every book.
  • Amazon processes your login and your use of its page under its own responsibility. Amazon's privacy notice applies. PageBite sends nothing to Amazon beyond what the browser window sends when it opens Amazon's page.
  • To keep AI costs in check, our server counts how many batches you imported in the last 7 days, as for the scan limit (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You start the import yourself.
